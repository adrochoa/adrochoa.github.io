Here is a demonstration of the noct salamander piano from the accurate salamander project.
It is a free sfz sample library and instrument from a Yamaha C5 grand piano.
I think it sounds great, especially for being absolutely free.
This is me playing the Sarabande from J.S. Bach's French Suite No. 4 in E♭ major, BWV 815.
Enjoy!
