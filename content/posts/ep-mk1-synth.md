The title says it all again.
Here is a port of EP-MK1 to Faust (Thanks to Claude Sonnet 5.5).
The original project and my Faust gist are linked below, along with information about Faust as a programming language.
This one sounds a lot like the original. It's a little quiet, but I like the way it turned out. I'm sure it could be tweaked.
A sound sample of my playing of the port are here!
Not perfect, but a nice tone.
